MATCHBIRD - Receipt Matching Application

Python • OCR • Automation

The Challenge

A construction bookkeeper was spending 8+ hours weekly manually matching hundreds of credit card receipts to Buildertrend transactions. The process was tedious, error-prone, and pulled them away from higher-value work.

The Solution

Built a desktop application using Python, PySide6, and OCR technology that automatically matches receipts to transactions. The app scans receipts, extracts key data, and pairs them with Buildertrend entries in seconds.

The Results

  • ✅ Reduced processing time from 8 hours to 30 minutes weekly
  • ✅ Eliminated manual data entry errors
  • ✅ Saved $400+/month in bookkeeping costs
  • ✅ Freed up time for strategic financial analysis
