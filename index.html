Hey, I'm Christin! 👋

I started Sunnyside Ops with a simple mission: help small businesses stop drowning in repetitive tasks and actually enjoy running their companies again.

My journey began when I jumped in to help my family's construction business catch up on a year of financial reconciliation. What started as a daughter helping out turned into a passion for solving operational puzzles with code.

Now I specialize in building custom automation solutions that actually make sense for small businesses – no enterprise prices, no overwhelming features you'll never use, just smart tools that give you your time back.

When I'm not automating workflows or wrestling with APIs, you'll find me exploring new ways to make technology accessible for businesses who've been told it's "too complicated" or "too expensive" for them.
